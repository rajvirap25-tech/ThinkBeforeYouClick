GET TO KNOW AI

Understand the
intelligence behind
your screen.

Artificial intelligence is software that learns patterns from data to make predictions, generate content, or help with tasks.

✦

AI can be helpful, but it is not always correct. Think critically and verify important information.

Voice assistantsFace recognitionChatbotsTranslation toolsRecommendations
How do we use AI every day?

It helps translate a sentence, answer a voice command, recognise a face, or suggest the next song.

How does AI influence social media?

Recommendation systems look for patterns in activity to predict which posts someone might watch or engage with.

What can AI help us do?

AI can support learning, improve accessibility through captions, translate languages, and help people brainstorm.

What are its limitations?

AI can make mistakes, reflect bias in its data, and create convincing false content. Check original sources and protect private information.

CLICK WITH CONFIDENCE

Stay smart. Stay safe online.

⌑

Keep personal things private

Check your audience before posting photos or personal details. Keep your address and live location private.

✳

Strengthen your sign-in

Use a long, unique password for each account and turn on two-step verification when available.

⌕

Check before you share

Look for the original source, publication date, and supporting evidence. Compare with trusted sources.

↗

Pause on unfamiliar links

Check the sender and website address. Open the official website yourself when a message feels suspicious.

♡

Recognise cyberbullying

Repeated threats, humiliation, or unwanted messages deserve support. Save evidence, block, and tell a trusted person.

⚑

Use your reporting tools

Report harmful or inappropriate posts through the platform. Ask a trusted adult for help when you need it.
